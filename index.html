User Experience

The Circular Journey

01

Write

Use the BloomPen standardly for sketching, note-taking, and capturing ideas until it reaches its stub.

02

Plant

Insert the green capsule stub directly into moist soil at a 45-degree angle in sunlight.

03

Grow

Water regularly. The capsule dissolves within 48 hours, sprouting vibrant herbs or flora.
